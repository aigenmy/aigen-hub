# Service Level Agreement

This agreement applies to companies on the **Business** and **Enterprise** plans once payments are switched on.

## 1. Availability

AIGen Hub aims for **99.5 %** availability each calendar month, measured on the app at app.aigen.my and its message-receiving endpoints.

Not counted: planned maintenance announced at least 48 hours ahead (at most 4 hours a month, outside 9 am – 6 pm Malaysia time), outages of connected platforms (such as Meta, Telegram or email providers), problems caused by your systems or by use against our policies, and events beyond our reasonable control.

## 2. Service credits

| Monthly availability | Credit on that month's fee |
|---|---|
| below 99.5 % | 5 % |
| below 99.0 % | 10 % |
| below 97.0 % | 25 % |

Ask for a credit within 30 days of the end of the month. Credits are applied to your next invoice and are your only remedy for missed availability.

## 3. Support response times

| Priority | Example | First response |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent | Nobody in your company can use the inbox | 2 business hours |
| High | One channel stopped receiving messages | 4 business hours |
| Normal | A question or a smaller problem | 1 business day |

Business hours: Monday to Friday, 9 am – 6 pm Malaysia time, excluding public holidays in Kuala Lumpur.
