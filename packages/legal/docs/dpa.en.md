# Data Processing Agreement

This agreement forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between **{{legal_name}}** ({{registration_no}}) ("AIGen", the processor) and the company that uses AIGen Hub ("Customer", the controller). It is accepted by the Customer's Owner on the Customer's behalf.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This agreement covers personal data that the Customer puts into AIGen Hub or receives through it, such as its customers' names, phone numbers, messages, files, contact details and invoices ("Customer Personal Data").

1.2 The Customer decides why and how Customer Personal Data is processed. AIGen processes it only to provide AIGen Hub.

## 2. Instructions

2.1 AIGen processes Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Customer's use and configuration of AIGen Hub are its instructions.

2.2 AIGen will tell the Customer if it believes an instruction breaks the law.

## 3. Confidentiality

Everyone at AIGen who can access Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality. AIGen staff open a Customer's workspace only when the Customer asks for help or to investigate abuse; every such access is recorded and shown to the Customer in Settings › Security.

## 4. Security

AIGen maintains appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest;
- separation between companies enforced by database row-level security and tested automatically;
- 2-step sign-in for all AIGen staff, and available to every user;
- least-privilege access and an append-only activity log of sensitive actions;
- secrets such as channel tokens stored encrypted and never shown again after entry;
- backups and a tested restore procedure.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Customer authorises AIGen to use the sub-processors listed on the [Sub-processors](/legal/subprocessors) page.

5.2 AIGen will give at least 30 days' notice of a new sub-processor. The Customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds; if no solution is found, the Customer may end the affected service and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees.

5.3 AIGen imposes data-protection terms on each sub-processor that are no less protective than this agreement.

## 6. Transfers

Customer Personal Data is stored in Singapore. Some sub-processors process data in other countries. AIGen transfers data only where permitted by the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, relying on substantially similar protection or appropriate safeguards, and keeps a transfer impact assessment for each destination.

## 7. Helping the Customer

7.1 AIGen gives the Customer tools to export, correct and delete Customer Personal Data, and assists with requests from data subjects that the Customer cannot handle with those tools.

7.2 AIGen provides reasonable information to help the Customer with data protection impact assessments.

## 8. Personal data breaches

AIGen will notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any case within **48 hours** of becoming aware of a breach affecting Customer Personal Data, with the information available at the time, and will help the Customer meet its own notification duties.

## 9. Return and deletion

9.1 The Owner may export the Customer's data at any time.

9.2 After the subscription ends, AIGen keeps Customer Personal Data for 30 days for export and then deletes it. Backups are overwritten within a further 35 days. AIGen keeps only what the law requires it to keep.

## 10. Audits

AIGen will make available the information reasonably needed to show compliance with this agreement. Where that is not enough, the Customer may request one audit per year, at its own cost, with 30 days' notice and under confidentiality.

## 11. Liability and term

Liability under this agreement is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service. This agreement lasts as long as AIGen processes Customer Personal Data.
