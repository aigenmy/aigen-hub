# Sub-processors

These service providers help us run AIGen Hub. We give 30 days' notice before adding a new one (see the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa)).

| Provider | Purpose | Data location |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in, file storage, real-time updates | Singapore |
| Vercel | Application hosting | Singapore, United States |
| Inngest | Background jobs | United States |
| Upstash | Rate limiting | Singapore |
| Resend | Service emails (invitations, alerts) | United States |
| Sentry | Error reports (personal data removed) | United States |
| Anthropic | AI Assistant | United States |
| OpenAI | AI Assistant, search embeddings | United States |
| Google | AI Assistant, Google sign-in | United States, Singapore |
| OpenRouter and the model hosts it routes to | AI Assistant (selected tasks) | Varies by model; listed in the app |
| Curlec (Razorpay) | Subscription payments, once payments are on | Malaysia |
| Meta | WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger channels you connect | United States, global |

Channels you connect (Meta, Telegram, TikTok, your email provider) process messages under their own terms as your providers, not ours.
