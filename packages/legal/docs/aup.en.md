# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to everyone who uses AIGen Hub. Breaking it may lead to suspension under the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Messaging rules

- Send marketing messages only to people who agreed to receive them, and keep proof of that agreement.
- Honour every request to stop, at once.
- Follow the policies of each platform you connect, including the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy, Instagram and Facebook policies, TikTok and Telegram rules.
- WhatsApp may not be used for a general-purpose AI chatbot. The AI assistant in AIGen Hub must stay a support for your own business, and a person must always be reachable.
- Do not pretend to be another person or business.

## 2. Content you must not send or store

- Anything unlawful in Malaysia or in your customer's country.
- Scams, phishing, pyramid schemes or misleading offers.
- Malware or links to it.
- Harassment, threats, hate speech, or content that sexualises minors.
- Personal data you have no right to use.

## 3. Businesses we do not serve

Unlicensed gambling, unlicensed financial services or money lending, illegal drugs, weapons, adult services, counterfeit goods, and any business that needs a licence it does not have.

## 4. Security and fair use

- Do not try to access another company's data or bypass limits.
- Do not test AIGen Hub's security without our written permission. Report weaknesses to {{contact_email}}.
- Do not overload the service, scrape it, or resell it without an agreement with us.

## 5. What we do

We may remove content, pause a channel, or suspend an account that breaks this policy. Where reasonable, we will tell you first and give you a chance to fix the problem.
