# Cookie Notice

AIGen Hub uses only cookies that are needed for the service to work. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies in the app.

| Cookie | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| sb-…-auth-token | Keeps you signed in (set by our sign-in provider, Supabase) | Until you sign out, refreshed while you use the app |
| hub_locale | Remembers your language | 1 year |
| hub_company | Remembers which company you are working in | 1 year |

Because these cookies are strictly necessary, the app does not ask for consent to use them. If you block them, you will not be able to sign in.
