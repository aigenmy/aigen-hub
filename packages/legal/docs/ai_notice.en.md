# AI Notice

AIGen Hub includes an AI assistant ("AIGen Assistant"). This notice explains what it does and what it does not do.

## 1. What the Assistant does

- **Suggest mode:** it drafts a reply. A person chooses to use, edit or reject it.
- **Auto mode** (only if your company switches it on): it replies by itself within the rules you set, and hands the conversation to a person for complaints, prices outside your list, requests to speak to a person, or when it is not confident.
- It can summarise conversations and help write content in Bahasa Malaysia, English and Chinese.

## 2. It can be wrong

AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date. Drafts are suggestions. A person in your company is responsible for what is sent from your accounts. The Assistant does not make decisions with legal effect, such as refusing service or setting prices outside your price list.

## 3. Who processes the text

To produce a reply, the relevant part of the conversation and the company documents you allowed are sent to AI model providers listed on our [Sub-processors](/legal/subprocessors) page. They may process it outside Malaysia. They are not allowed to use it to train their models.

## 4. No training on your data

We do not use your conversations, documents or customer data to train any AI model.

## 5. Records

For each AI action we keep a record of when it ran, which feature was used and its cost, without the message content, for up to 13 months. The suggestion itself stays in the conversation history like any other message.

## 6. Telling your customers

We recommend that you tell customers when they are talking to an AI assistant, for example in your welcome message, and some platforms require it.
