# Refund Policy

- **Beta:** AIGen Hub is free during the beta, so there is nothing to refund.
- **Monthly plans:** not refundable. If you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the month you paid for.
- **Yearly plans:** if you cancel within 14 days of your first yearly payment, we refund the unused months. After 14 days, yearly plans are not refundable.
- **Add-ons** (extra seats, AI assist packs, modules): not refundable.
- **Our mistakes:** if we charged you wrongly, we refund the difference in full.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 14 days. To ask for one, email {{support_email}} from the Owner's email address.
