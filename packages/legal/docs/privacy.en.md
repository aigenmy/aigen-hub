# Privacy Notice

This notice explains how **{{legal_name}}** ({{registration_no}}) ("AIGen", "we") handles personal data in AIGen Hub, under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 as amended in 2024. A Bahasa Malaysia version will be published alongside this English version.

## 1. Our two roles

- **For your account** (your name, email, sign-in and company details), AIGen is the **data user (controller)**. This notice covers that data.
- **For your customers' data** (contacts, conversations, files and invoices you put into AIGen Hub), your company is the controller and AIGen is the **data processor**. We process it only on your company's instructions, as set out in the [Data Processing Agreement](/legal/dpa).

## 2. What we collect

- Account details: name, email, password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), language, time zone, profile picture if you add one.
- Company details: company name, type of business, registration numbers you enter, members and their roles.
- Security and usage records: sign-in times, IP address, browser, the actions you take (the activity log), 2-step sign-in status.
- Messages you send to our support.
- Billing details once payments are switched on (handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers).

## 3. Why we use it

- To provide AIGen Hub and keep your account and company workspace working (contract).
- To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and investigate incidents (legitimate interest and legal obligation).
- To send service emails, such as invitations, security alerts and changes to our terms (contract).
- To bill you once payments are on, and to keep tax records (contract and legal obligation).
- To send product news, only if you agree; you can withdraw at any time.

## 4. Who we share it with

We use service providers (sub-processors) to run AIGen Hub, listed on our [Sub-processors](/legal/subprocessors) page with their purpose and region. We share personal data with authorities only when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Transfers outside Malaysia

AIGen Hub's database is hosted in **Singapore**. Some providers, such as AI model providers, process data in other countries (for example the United States or the European Union). We transfer data only where the destination has substantially similar protection, or under safeguards such as contractual commitments, and we keep a transfer impact assessment for each destination.

## 6. How long we keep it

- Account data: while your account is active, and up to 90 days after you close it.
- Activity log entries: for your plan's retention period (90 days to 3 years).
- Security records: up to 13 months.
- Billing and tax records: 7 years, as required by Malaysian law.

## 7. How we protect it

Encryption in transit and at rest, strict separation between companies enforced in the database, 2-step sign-in (required for AIGen staff), least-privilege access, an activity log of sensitive actions, and regular backups.

## 8. Your rights

You may ask to access or correct your personal data, withdraw consent, limit processing, and receive a copy of your data in a portable format. Most of this you can do yourself in Settings. For anything else, email our Data Protection Officer. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

## 10. Data breaches

If a breach of personal data is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours and affected people without undue delay, as the law requires.

## 11. Cookies

We use only cookies needed to sign you in, remember your language and your selected company. See our [Cookie Notice](/legal/cookies).

## 12. Children

AIGen Hub is for businesses and is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 13. Changes

We will tell you about important changes in the app and ask you to accept the new version at your next sign-in.
